What Reclaim reads

One file of the payments you made out of {{system}}: a CSV or an Excel workbook (.xlsx). Title lines at the top, totals, and reports grouped by vendor are fine, so you shouldn’t need to edit it first. Uploading a file connects nothing to your account and changes nothing inside it.

If your headings are ones Reclaim knows, it reads them straight away. If not, it shows you your own columns and asks which is which. Three are required. Each of the others turns on more checks.

Required

Every payment needs

  • Vendor
  • Payment date
  • Amount paid

Add if you have them

More checks

  • Invoice number: duplicate payments
  • Invoice amount: overpayments
  • Invoice date and payment terms: early-payment discounts
  • Bank account, last 4 digits: changed bank details
