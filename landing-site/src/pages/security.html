What it reads

What Reclaim reads.

  • 01The source you choose

    You export the payments you want checked and upload them as a CSV or Excel file. A direct QuickBooks Online import is in testing and isn’t open to customers yet.

  • 02Yours to take or delete

    From Settings you can download everything in your account, or delete the account and the audits in it.

  • 03Saved when you sign in

    The checks run in your browser. Once you’re signed in, your audit is saved to Reclaim’s server so you can come back to it. Your team records the accounting in your own books.

  • 04No payment rights

    Reclaim cannot move money or release a payment. Refunds and credits go straight to you; Reclaim never holds recovered funds.

Evidence

Every result points at a record.

  • 01Traced to the source

    Every finding names the records it rests on, where each one came from in your books or your file, and the fields that matched.

  • 02Shown side by side

    The bill and the payment sit side by side with the differences marked, not as a figure you have to trust.

  • 03Yours to check

    You can open any record behind a result and check it against your books or the export you uploaded.

  • 04Yours to decide

    Nothing goes to a vendor until you approve the recovery, and every step taken after that is logged: who, what, when and why.

Never

What Reclaim never gets.

Three things Reclaim has no use for, and is built never to have.

  • 01Your passwords

    Reclaim never asks for your password to your accounting system, your bank or a vendor portal. The QuickBooks Online import in testing uses Intuit’s own sign-in. Intuit’s permission allows changes; Reclaim only sends read requests.

  • 02Payment authority

    Nothing in Reclaim can approve, release or reverse a payment. Whoever holds that authority today still holds it afterwards.

  • 03Your ledger, in an AI prompt

    AI doesn’t find the mistakes or work out the numbers. When you ask for an AI draft, it gets that one case: the vendor, the finding, the invoices and payments in it, their terms and the amount. It also gets your note, and the name, business name and email address in your sender details.

Reporting a problem

Found something?

If you think you have found a security problem with this site or with Reclaim, tell us before you tell anyone else. Use the contact form and say that it is a security report.

We don’t run a bug bounty programme, and we would rather say that plainly than imply one exists.
