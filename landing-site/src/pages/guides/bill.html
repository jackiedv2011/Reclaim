Exporting from BILL

One list of payments, exported and uploaded as it is.

Before you begin

What you need

A BILL role with reporting permission.

A Reclaim account, to upload the file.

Your screen may differ

Menus and report names change between plans and over time. If yours don’t match, look for any report that lists the payments you made, or ask us.

01

Run the bill payments report

Open the Reports section and run the bill payments report for the period you want checked. For a single vendor, you can use the Payments tab on the vendor’s record instead.

Keep the vendor, the payment date, the amount, and the vendor’s invoice number.

02

Export it

Export the report as CSV or Excel. Either one can be uploaded as it is.

# in BILL
Reports → bill payments → export
