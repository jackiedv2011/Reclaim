Exporting from QuickBooks Online

One list of payments, exported and uploaded as it is.

Before you begin

What you need

A QuickBooks Online login that can run and export reports.

A Reclaim account, to upload the file.

Your screen may differ

Menus and report names change between plans and over time. If yours don’t match, look for any report that lists the payments you made, or ask us.

01

Run a payments report

Open Reports, then Standard reports, and search for a report that lists transactions by vendor, such as Transaction List by Vendor. Filter it to bill payments, and set the period you want checked.

Report names and columns vary by plan. If the report lets you choose columns, include the vendor, date, amount and invoice number.

02

Export it

With the report open, choose Export/Print, then Export to Excel. QuickBooks saves an Excel file, which you can upload as it is.

# in QuickBooks Online
Reports → Standard reports → your report
Export/Print → Export to Excel
