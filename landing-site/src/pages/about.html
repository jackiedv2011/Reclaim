About

Money that leaves by mistake leaves a record.

We build software that reads that record, shows you what it found, and helps you ask for the money back.

A business pays the same invoice twice. Nobody meant to. One copy came by email, the other by post, and the numbers differ by a dash.

It isn’t unusual. APQC’s benchmark puts duplicate or erroneous payments at 1.5% of disbursements at the median organization.

Both payments are still in the books, a few rows apart. Someone has to look, and then someone has to ask.

That’s the job we built Reclaim to do.

What we hold to

Five things we won’t trade.

  1. Show the row.

    Every finding names the payments behind it and what matched. If we can’t point at it, we don’t claim it.

  2. A flag isn’t a debt.

    A match is a reason to look. You check it against your bank and your bills before it becomes a request.

  3. Ask for less access.

    You can start with a file you export yourself. Reclaim can’t move money and never writes to your books.

  4. You decide.

    Nothing goes to a vendor until you approve it. What to raise and what to leave alone is your call.

  5. Count what came back.

    A promised refund isn’t money back. It counts when you record that it arrived, or that the credit was used.

Where we are

What works, and what doesn’t yet.

  1. Today

    Upload, check, ask, track

    Eight checks on an export of your payments, with the evidence for each finding. A proven duplicate gets a drafted request, your approval, and a case you follow to the end.

  2. In testing

    QuickBooks Online import

    Bringing payments in directly instead of by file. It isn’t open to customers yet.

  3. Later

    More than your ledger

    Supplier statements and your bank, so Reclaim can find credits your books never recorded and confirm refunds for itself.
