# Export guides: what is confirmed (vendor steps checked 2026-11-24, upload steps 2026-11-25)

What Reclaim reads and asks is confirmed on the upload screen with made-up files shaped like each export (CSV and .xlsx).
The vendor steps are confirmed only where listed here.

## QuickBooks Online
- Confirmed (Intuit, "Export reports to Excel"): Reports, Standard reports, pick a report, Export/Print, Export to Excel.
- Not confirmed: that Transaction List by Vendor carries the vendor's invoice number, and the exact column names.

## Xero
- Nothing confirmed: Xero Central asks for a login. The guide names no menu path or report on purpose.

## NetSuite
- Confirmed (Oracle, "Exporting Search Results"): Export - CSV, and the Export Lists and Perform Search permissions.
- Confirmed path to a new saved search: Lists, Search, Saved Searches, New (from a Fixed Assets article).
- Not confirmed: the bill payment criteria and which result columns carry the vendor's invoice number.

## BILL
- Confirmed from article summaries only: the Reports section includes bill payments and needs reporting permission;
  a vendor record's Payments tab exports to CSV or Excel.
- Not confirmed: button labels and column names (the full articles did not load).
